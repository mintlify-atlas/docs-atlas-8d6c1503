Open SSTP Client brings the Microsoft Secure Socket Tunneling Protocol (MS-SSTP) to Android, letting you establish encrypted VPN tunnels to servers running VPN Azure Cloud or SoftEther VPN Server. Configure your connection once, save it as a profile, and connect from the home screen or the Quick Settings tile.

## How it works

Open SSTP Client implements the MS-SSTP protocol entirely on-device. When you connect, the app opens an SSL/TLS connection to your server, performs an HTTP upgrade handshake to establish the SSTP tunnel, negotiates PPP layer settings, and brings up a VPN interface that routes your traffic securely.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Install Open SSTP Client from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kittoku.osc) or download an APK from the [releases page](https://github.com/kittoku/Open-SSTP-Client/releases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your server details">
    Open the app and enter your **Host**, **Username**, and **Password** on the Home tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Toggle the switch to **On**. When a key icon appears in the status bar, the VPN is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize (optional)">
    Adjust SSL settings, PPP protocols, routing rules, and more in the **Setting** tab. Save your configuration as a named profile for quick reuse.
  </Step>
</Steps>
