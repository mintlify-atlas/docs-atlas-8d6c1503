---

Open SSTP Client connects to MS-SSTP VPN servers through an encrypted TLS tunnel on port 443. This guide walks you through entering your credentials, making your first connection, and disconnecting — all from the app's Home and Setting tabs.

<Note>
Before connecting, confirm that your VPN server has DHCP enabled. The app cannot assign an IP address without it.
</Note>

## Connect to your VPN

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and go to the Home tab">
    Launch Open SSTP Client. The **Home** tab is displayed by default. You will see fields for **Host**, **Username**, and **Password**, and a toggle switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your server hostname">
    Tap the **Host** field and enter your VPN server's hostname or IP address.

    <Warning>
      Enter only the hostname or IP address — do not include a port number or `http://` prefix. For example, use `vpn.example.com`, not `vpn.example.com:443`. The port is configured separately in the Setting tab (default: 443).
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your username and password">
    Fill in the **Username** and **Password** fields with your VPN account credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the VPN">
    Tap the toggle switch to **On**. The app will begin establishing the connection. The status field updates as the connection progresses through SSL handshake, SSTP, and PPP negotiation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection is active">
    When the VPN is connected, a **key icon** appears in the Android status bar. The ongoing VPN notification also appears in the notification drawer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect from the VPN

You can disconnect in two ways:

- **Home tab**: Tap the toggle switch to turn it **Off**
- **Notification**: Pull down the notification drawer and tap **DISCONNECT** in the ongoing VPN notification

## Use the Quick Settings tile

Open SSTP Client includes a Quick Settings tile so you can toggle the VPN without opening the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the tile">
    Pull down the Quick Settings panel, tap **Edit** (or the pencil icon), and drag the **Open SSTP Client** tile into your active tiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle from Quick Settings">
    Tap the tile to connect or disconnect. The tile shows the current VPN state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save your configuration

Once your connection is working, save it as a profile so you don't have to re-enter credentials next time.

<Info>
Go to the upper-right option menu and tap **Save Profile**. You can save multiple profiles for different servers and switch between them quickly. See the [Profiles guide](/guides/profiles) for details.
</Info>
